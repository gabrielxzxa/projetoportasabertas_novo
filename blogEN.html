Types of IT Courses & Universities

The table below details 12 degree options in technology and related fields, ranging from
short-term specialized degree programs (2.5 years) to traditional bachelor's degrees.

Course Degree Type Duration Main Focus
1. Systems Analysis and Development Technologist 2.5 years Creation and maintenance of applications, operating systems, and enterprise software
2. Digital Games Technologist 2.5 years Creation of games for various platforms and reality simulators
3. Software Engineering Bachelor's 4 years Construction and maintenance of software with a strong foundation in engineering and quality control
4. Information Technology Management Technologist 2.5 years Coordination of IT teams and management of services and software
5. Computer Networks Technologist 2.5 years Management, optimization, and security of networks and data communication devices.
6. Internet Systems Technologist 2.5 years Development, management, and usability of platforms, websites, and web portals.
7. Data Science and Behavioral Analysis Technologist 2.5 years Business Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, and Big Data for decision- making.
8. Databases Technologist 2.5 years Administration, maintenance, and security of large volumes of corporate data.
9. Library Science Bachelor's Degree 3 years Management of scientific information and physical or digital collections.
10. Cybersecurity Technologist Degree 2.5 years (5 semesters) Protection of data and financial transactions, and mitigation of virtual threats.
11. Mobile App Development Technologist Degree 2.5 years Creation of digital solutions focused on smartphones and tablets.
12. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Technologist Degree 2.5 years Computer vision, neural networks, and cognitive computing.
Universities
USP (University of São Paulo): Consistently at the top of national indicators for teaching and research quality in computing.
Unicamp (University of Campinas): Consistently achieves the highest possible score on Enade and maintains strong ties to the Campinas technology hub.
UFMG (Federal University of Minas Gerais): Nationally recognized for the excellence of its computer science department.
UFRGS (Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul): A long-standing benchmark, achieving top scores in Ministry of Education (MEC) evaluations.
UFPE (Federal University of Pernambuco): Stands out significantly due to the Porto Digital innovation ecosystem in Recife.
ITA (Aeronautics Institute of Technology): Leads specific excellence rankings focused on engineering and high-tech fields.
